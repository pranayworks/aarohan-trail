How it works

STEP 1

Choose where to go

Pick up and drop off in Lilongwe. Add up to two stops. The fare is shown before you book.

STEP 2

Meet your driver

A nearby approved driver accepts. You see their name, vehicle and plate. Share your 4-digit PIN only after you board.

STEP 3

Ride and pay

Follow your trip live, then pay your driver in cash. Rate your driver when you arrive.

Built for safety

Approved drivers

Drivers register with their documents and are reviewed before they can take trips.

Safety PIN

A trip only starts when the driver enters the PIN shown on your phone.

SOS button

One tap alerts the Aarohan safety team with your location. In immediate danger, always call 997.

Parcel delivery

Send a parcel across Lilongwe. The recipient confirms delivery with a code.

Drive with Aarohan

Register in the driver app, upload your licence, ID and vehicle documents, and start taking trips once you are approved.

Payment

During the pilot, trips are paid in cash to the driver. Mobile money payments are planned for a later release.
