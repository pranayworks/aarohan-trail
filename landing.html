A city should be easy to cross.

You see the fare before you book.

Pickup, drop-off, price. If the price is not right for you, you never book. Prices do not rise because it is busy.

You know who is coming.

The driver's name, vehicle and number plate show before they arrive. A four-digit PIN means only the right person starts your trip.

Every driver is checked by a person.

Identity, licence, photos of the vehicle and number plate, and a live face check. Someone on our team reviews each application before a driver can take a trip.

Parcels need a code to be delivered.

The receiver gives the driver a one-time code. Without it, the delivery cannot be completed. The driver calls you or our support instead.

Help is one tap away.

The SOS button sends your trip and location to our safety desk. The police number is always one tap from the same screen.

How a trip works.

Choose where to go.

Set your pickup and drop-off in Lilongwe. Add up to two stops. The route and the fare appear before you confirm.

A driver accepts.

A nearby approved driver takes the trip. You see their name, vehicle and plate, and you can follow them on the map.

Share your PIN.

When the driver arrives, tell them your four-digit PIN. The trip only starts when it matches.

Ride, pay, rate.

Pay your driver in cash when you arrive, then rate the trip. Both of you can see what happened later.

Built in Hyderabad. Made for Lilongwe.

Aarohan (आरोहण) is the word for ascent, the climb you make when you take the next step. It is also our promise: rise, build, grow, together with the city we serve.

We build the software in Hyderabad, India, and we start with Lilongwe, Malawi. We chose to begin with cash, with plain screens, and with checks that a person makes, because trust has to be earned one trip at a time.
