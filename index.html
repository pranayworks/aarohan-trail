Integrated Malawi Financial & Transit Architecture

The system connects passenger demand with driver dispatch using a lightweight peer-to-peer event bus (via native BroadcastChannel and localStorage sync). All monetary settlements simulate integration with Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, and the Reserve Bank of Malawi (RBM) National Payment System. Commercial transit licenses and safety protocols adhere to the Directorate of Road Traffic and Safety Services (DRTSS).
